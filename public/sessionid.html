Pairing code
  1. Open WhatsApp on the phone with this number.
  2. Go to Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.
  3. Tap Link with phone number instead and enter the code.
  4. Wait a few seconds. Your Session ID arrives as a WhatsApp message. Tap its Copy code button.

Never share your Session ID. It gives full access to your WhatsApp. It stays valid for 24 hours, so deploy your bot before it expires.
